About Me

I am a Computer Science student at The University of Hong Kong, focused on AI systems, robotics, and sports technology. My long-term goal is to build AI that enhances human performance—systems that can understand intent, provide real-time guidance, and help people make better decisions in both digital and physical environments.

I’ve worked across embodied AI, multimodal interfaces, and bioinformatics: building Vision-Language-Action pipelines for fine-grained robotic manipulation, a multimodal “Speak-to-Draw” analytics system, a robotics control bridge for a Franka arm, and a pipeline for detecting A-to-I RNA editing from nanopore signals. Outside the lab, I’m the starting quarterback and captain for the Hong Kong Cobras American football team and previously served as Vice President of CSSA HKU’s Club Management Department.

I love working at the intersection of research and real-world systems—turning ideas into working prototypes, and prototypes into products that people actually use.

Research Experience

I’ve contributed to four research projects spanning embodied AI, human–computer interaction, robotics, and bioinformatics.

XLANG Lab, HKU — Vision-Language-Action General Policy

ICML/CoRL 2026 Target

Advisor: Prof. Tao Yu

  • Working on generalist VLA policies that follow fine-grained, multi-step robotic manipulation commands instead of only coarse task labels.
  • Built an LLM-assisted data pipeline that discovers open-source manipulation datasets, filters trajectories, and generates first-pass sub-action annotations from task descriptions, videos, and state sequences.
  • Designed the system to reduce annotation time from minutes to seconds and scale toward 2M+ fine-grained trajectories.

VIA Lab, UC Davis — Speak-to-Draw Analytics Tool

EuroVis 2026 Target

Advisor: Prof. Dongyu Liu

  • Developing “Speak-to-Draw,” a multimodal system where users explore time-series data via natural language and interactive visualizations.
  • Co-designed the interaction flow from speech input to query parsing to visual refinement, bridging HCI with backend analytics.
  • Implementing components across frontend, backend, and model integration to support flexible, user-centered exploratory analysis.

MEDCVR Lab, University of Toronto — Franka Toolbox Bridge

Mitacs Globalink Scholar

Advisor: Prof. Lueder Kahrs

  • Built franka_toolbox_bridge, a framework that exposes safe, high-level APIs for controlling a Franka Emika arm from Python and C++.
  • Abstracted motion primitives, control loops, and error handling to make real-world robot experiments easier and safer to prototype.
  • Integrated the bridge into UTM’s CSC376H5 course, enabling students to run hands-on robotics labs on real hardware.

Bioinformatics Lab, HKU — A-to-I Editing with Nanopore

Ongoing

Advisor: Prof. Ruibang Luo

  • Exploring methods to detect A-to-I RNA editing using electrical signal data from nanopore sequencing.
  • Working on benchmarking pipelines that compare different signal-level and basecalling-based approaches on real datasets.
  • Aiming to better connect long-read sequencing signals with RNA editing biology.
