4. XLANG – Large-Scale VLA Dataset Curation & Annotation Pipeline

HKU XLANG Lab —2025

4.1 Overview

In the XLANG Lab, I contribute to the CPT (Coarse-to-Fine Pretraining) & Sim2Real team, which aims to build a scalable, high-quality Vision-Language-Action (VLA) dataset for training the next generation of robotic agents. The project integrates:

dataset collection & filtering,

metadata standardization,

fine-grained instruction rewriting,

semi-automated annotation with LLMs,

and Sim2Real-aligned preprocessing.

Our goal is to convert raw, diverse robot datasets into a unified format usable for large-scale pretraining and evaluation.

4.2.4 Fine-Grained Instruction Annotation (LLM-Driven Human+AI Pipeline)

I designed and implemented the first-pass LLM-based instruction annotation pipeline to automate fine-grained labeling across large robotic datasets. Using models such as Gemini, the system:

generates step-level action sequences from raw videos,

rewrites coarse template instructions into fine-grained, actionable substeps,

identifies trajectory-level differences across demonstrations, and

produces consistent multi-view annotations without manual alignment.

This reduces annotation cost dramatically: LLMs generate structured labels automatically, and human annotators only perform lightweight refinement and verification rather than annotating from scratch.

The resulting workflow scales to thousands of trajectories and significantly improves the efficiency of building high-quality VLA training data.
